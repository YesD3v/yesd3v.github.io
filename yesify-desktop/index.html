    The biggest update yet: all of YouTube Music, and a smoother app everywhere.

    Full release notes ›

    Search all of YouTube Music

    Songs, artists, albums and playlists, right next to the matches from your own library. No account needed.

    Artist & album pages

    Top songs and every song, albums, singles, videos, playlists and similar artists. Save albums and playlists to your library.

    Explore

    New albums & singles, trending songs, charts, top artists, and moods & genres full of playlists.

    Radio & Mixes

    Start an endless radio from any song or artist. Go to the artist or album of anything playing, and go Back.

    Lyrics that follow you

    Ready before you press play. Scroll away and they wait; two seconds later they glide back to the line being sung.

    Glass & smooth

    Instant glass loading screens with a sweeping shine, smooth scrolling everywhere, and a cache size you choose.

    Everything you'd expect

    And a few things you wouldn't.

    No account, no adsUnlimited skips, nothing to sign up for, no telemetry.
    Your Spotify playlistsPaste a playlist, album or song link and play it, however long it is.
    Your libraryLiked Songs and your own playlists with covers, kept on your PC.
    Word-by-word lyricsApple Music-style sweep, timed to the exact audio. Click a line to jump.
    Home made for youDaily mixes and picks built from what you actually play.
    Shuffle that remembersSongs you haven't heard lately come first.
    MiniplayerAlways on top, freely resizable, colored from the cover.
    Discord & media keysRich Presence, the Windows media flyout and your keyboard's media keys.
    Plays from the trayClose the window and the music keeps going, using very little memory.

    Up and running in a minute

    DownloadGrab Yesify.exe and run it. One portable file: no installer, no .NET, nothing to set up.
    Let it runIf Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway (the exe isn't code-signed).
    Play anythingSearch YouTube Music, open Explore, or click Import Playlist and paste a Spotify link.

    Get Yesify

    Your library stays on your PC in %APPDATA%\Yesify.
