Yesify Desktop

A free, native music player for Windows. Paste a Spotify link and play — no account, no Premium, no ads.

Features

No Account, No Ads
Paste a public Spotify or YouTube link and play. Unlimited skips.
Synced Lyrics
Word-by-word, Apple Music style. Click a line to jump to it.
Your Library
Liked Songs, your own playlists with covers, and search.
Glass or Lightweight
Frosted glass over the artwork, or a flat look that uses ~7 MB.
Always-On-Top Mini Player
Freely resizable, colored from the album cover.
Discord & Media Keys
Rich Presence, the Windows media flyout and media keys.

Quick start

  1. Download Yesify.exe and run it. No installer, no setup.
  2. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info then Run anyway.
  3. Click Import Playlist and paste a Spotify playlist, album or song link.
