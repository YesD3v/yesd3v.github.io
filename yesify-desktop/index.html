    New in this version

    A whole new look: bolder, glassier, and faster everywhere.

    Full release notes ›

    A bolder, glassier look

    Vivid cover-colored backdrop, rounded glass panels, a floating player bar, thick icons and heavy type. And a new logo.

    Cinema

    The whole window becomes the song: a big cover, the title and controls, and huge lyrics that fade into the background.

    A smarter Library

    Every album your saved songs come from, filter chips, search that finds songs, grid or list, and pins to keep favorites on top.

    Your background

    The album cover or your own colors, tint and brightness, and an optional slow motion with a frame-rate cap.

    Faster and lighter

    Songs start sooner (they're looked up while you hover), pages open without stutter, and a lot less memory is used.

    Taskbar controls

    Previous, play / pause, next and mute right under the window preview when you hover Yesify in the taskbar.

    Everything you'd expect

    And a few things you wouldn't.

    No account, no adsUnlimited skips, nothing to sign up for, no telemetry.
    Your Spotify playlistsPaste a playlist, album or song link and play it, however long it is.
    Your libraryLiked Songs, your own playlists and every album you love, kept on your PC.
    Word-by-word lyricsApple Music-style sweep, timed to the exact audio. Click a line to jump.
    Home made for youDaily mixes and picks built from what you actually play.
    Shuffle that remembersSongs you haven't heard lately come first.
    Miniplayer & CinemaA tiny always-on-top player, or the whole window for the song and its lyrics.
    Discord & media keysRich Presence, the Windows media flyout and your keyboard's media keys.
    Plays from the trayClose the window and the music keeps going, using very little memory.

    Up and running in a minute

    DownloadGrab Yesify.exe and run it. One portable file: no installer, no .NET, nothing to set up.
    Let it runIf Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway (the exe isn't code-signed).
    Play anythingSearch YouTube Music, open Explore, or click Import in your Library and paste a Spotify link.

    Get Yesify

    Your library stays on your PC in %APPDATA%\Yesify.
